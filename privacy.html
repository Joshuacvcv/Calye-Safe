Privacy Notice

Your data, your rights

Short version of how Barangay Calye handles your information under RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).

What we collect

  • Account details: full name, email, address/barangay.
  • Verification: ID type and a photo of your valid ID.
  • Reports: incident details, photos, and GPS location.

Why we use it

  • To verify you are a real resident or responder.
  • To dispatch responders and track incidents.
  • To send barangay safety alerts.

Who sees it

  • Barangay staff and administrators (verification, dispatch, analytics).
  • Assigned responders see the incident and its location; reporter names are masked.
  • Map tiles, weather data, and app libraries technically receive connection details (like your IP address) — this is normal for any app and is not sold or shared further.

How long we keep it

  • Rejected ID photos are deleted after 30 days.
  • Old resolved reports are anonymized; accounts are deleted only on request or for cause.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct, or delete your data, or withdraw consent, through the Barangay Hall or our Data Protection Officer: [DPO name, contact]. Security incidents affecting your data will be communicated as required by law.
